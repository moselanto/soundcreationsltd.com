## Churches, mosques and other places of worship

Sound Creations designs and installs sound systems and acoustics for churches, mosques, temples and other worship spaces across Kenya. Whatever the faith, the brief is the same: every word heard clearly in every part of the hall, music or recitation that sounds natural, and a system your own team can run.

For church sound systems in Kenya, that usually means a PA designed around the room, stage monitoring, a mixer your volunteers understand and microphones for the pulpit, choir and band. Church AV systems can also include a separate audio feed for live streaming, set up depending on your needs. Mosque sound systems are usually built around speech: clear call to prayer, sermons that reach the back rows and the women's section, and simple controls for the imam or caretaker. Temple sound systems follow the same speech-first approach.

Equipment alone does not fix a room that echoes. Church acoustics, and the acoustics of any large worship hall, often need treatment first. See how we approached this at [PCEA Kahawa Farmers Church](/projects/pcea-kahawa-farmers/) with an acoustic ceiling, at [All Saints Cathedral](/projects/all-saints-cathedral/) with acoustic treatment and audio enhancement, and at [CITAM Buruburu](/projects/citam-buruburu/) with a full dB Technologies system.

Depending on your needs, we can:

- Visit the site to assess the room and your services
- Install equipment you have already bought
- Repair or service equipment we did not supply
- Set up audio for live streaming

## Sound, PA and acoustics for schools, auditoriums and theatres

Sound Creations provides school sound systems across Kenya, from public address for assemblies and announcements to complete auditorium sound systems for performances, prize-givings and worship. School AV systems work best when one team plans the hall, the speakers, the microphones and the controls together, so that staff and students can use them without a technician on site.

Auditorium sound systems need even coverage from the front row to the back wall, enough headroom for music and drama, and microphones that suit speakers, choirs and instruments. Theatre sound systems add stage monitoring, wireless microphones for performers and a mixing position with a clear view of the stage.

Good sound starts with the room. Many school halls have hard walls, tiled floors and high roofs, so theatre acoustics and auditorium acoustics are designed alongside the system. At [West Nairobi School](/projects/west-nairobi-school/) we combined a curved acoustic ceiling, treated side and back walls, acoustic seating and carpet with the sound and lighting design. For noise between classrooms, music rooms and halls, see [sound isolation](/solutions/sound-isolation/).

Typical scope:

- Public address and announcement systems for assemblies and grounds
- Hall and auditorium loudspeakers, mixers and microphones
- Theatre and drama sound, including wireless microphones
- Acoustic design and treatment for halls, chapels and auditoriums

### Extra FAQs (FAQ box currently has 2)

**What sound system does a school hall need?**
It depends on the size of the hall, how it is used and how much it echoes. Assemblies need clear speech; performances and worship need fuller sound and more microphones. We visit the hall, check its acoustics and design a system with simple controls for staff. Where the room echoes, treatment comes first so the system can work properly.

**Can one system handle assemblies, announcements and performances?**
Usually, yes. A well-designed school system can switch between simple public address for assemblies and announcements and a fuller set-up for concerts, drama and worship. We set up presets and clear controls so staff can change between them, and train staff and students at handover.

## 5. /solutions/acoustics/

### Append section

## Acoustics for studios, conference rooms and theatres

Room acoustics decide whether people can understand what is said. Speech intelligibility drops when sound keeps bouncing off hard surfaces, so words blur together even with good equipment. Sound Creations measures a room's reverberation (RT60) on site, sets a target for how the room will be used, designs the treatment, and measures again after installation to verify the result.

- **Studio acoustics:** recording, broadcast and podcast rooms need controlled reflections and even bass so that what you hear in the room is what goes on the recording. Treatment combines absorption, diffusion and bass control.
- **Conference room acoustics:** in boardrooms and meeting rooms, echo makes video calls tiring and microphones pick up the room as well as the voice. Treating the room improves clarity for people in the room and on the call. See [boardroom and conference room audio](/solutions/conferencing-boardroom-audio/).
- **Theatre acoustics:** theatres and auditoriums need speech and music to carry naturally to every seat, with treatment placed so the room still feels lively.

Acoustic treatment controls sound inside a room. It does not stop sound passing through walls, floors or ceilings to other rooms or neighbours. That is soundproofing, a separate job covered on our [sound isolation](/solutions/sound-isolation/) page.

Treatment products we supply and install include [Rockfon](/brands/rockfon/), [Barrisol](/brands/barrisol/), [Asona](/brands/asona/) and [Aid](/brands/aid/). See also [acoustic panels and ceilings](/solutions/acoustic-panels-ceilings/).

### Extra FAQs (FAQ box has CONTENT-DRAFTS D2 Q1-Q5; these are new)

**What is speech intelligibility and how is it improved?**
Speech intelligibility is how clearly listeners can understand spoken words in a room. It suffers when reverberation is long, background noise is high or loudspeakers are poorly placed. It is improved by reducing reverberation with acoustic treatment, controlling noise, and designing the sound system to cover listeners evenly. We measure the room before and after to confirm the improvement.

**Do studios need acoustic treatment or soundproofing?**
Often both, and they solve different problems. Treatment controls how sound behaves inside the studio so recordings and mixes are accurate. Soundproofing stops outside noise getting in and studio sound getting out. We assess both and recommend what the room needs; see our sound isolation page for soundproofing.

## What an AV and acoustic design package includes

Sound Creations provides audio visual consultancy and acoustic consultancy for architects, contractors, project managers and building owners in Kenya. We are most useful early, during design or before finishes go in, when acoustic and AV decisions cost least to change.

A typical sound system design and acoustic design package can include, depending on the project:

- **Brief and site survey:** how the space will be used, audience size, existing conditions and constraints.
- **Acoustic measurement:** on-site testing of reverberation (RT60) and background noise in existing rooms, using Rational Acoustics SMAART.
- **Acoustic criteria and design:** targets for reverberation and noise, and treatment or isolation recommendations, with materials and areas.
- **Sound system design:** loudspeaker positions and coverage modelled in EASE, microphone, mixing and control layouts, and signal flow.
- **Drawings and specifications:** equipment schedules and positions to coordinate with architectural, electrical and structural drawings.
- **Bill of quantities:** for tendering or budgeting.
- **Support through construction:** reviews, site visits, commissioning and verification measurements.

This AV consultancy can be delivered on its own for your contractor to tender, or followed by our own [integration](/service/integration/) and [installation](/solutions/installation/). See [acoustics](/solutions/acoustics/) for how measurement and treatment work.

## 7. /service/integration/

### Append section

## Audio visual integration in Kenya

Audio visual integration means making several systems work as one: sound, microphones, video, displays, lighting, control and acoustics, planned together so that one button or one operator can run the room. Equipment supply is buying the right products. AV integration is the design, programming and coordination that turns those products into a system that works reliably every day.

As an AV integration partner, Sound Creations takes responsibility across the whole chain:

- **Design:** how each system connects, where control sits and how the room's acoustics affect the result.
- **Coordination:** working with the architect, electrician, IT team and other trades on cabling, power and network.
- **Configuration:** mixers, processors, wireless microphones and control set up and tested together.
- **Commissioning:** tuning and verifying the whole system, not just each box.
- **Handover:** training, documentation and after-sales support.

Integrated audio-visual systems are common in churches that stream services, auditoriums that host events and lessons, and venues with several zones. At [West Nairobi School](/projects/west-nairobi-school/) acoustics, sound and lighting were designed as one, and at [CITAM Buruburu](/projects/citam-buruburu/) the sound system serves both the room and broadcast.

The physical work of mounting, cabling and commissioning is covered on our [installation](/solutions/installation/) page. Depending on your needs, we can also integrate equipment you already own.

### Extra FAQs (page currently shows no FAQ block; add if the FAQ box is enabled)

**What is the difference between AV integration and installation?**
Installation is the physical work: mounting, cabling, connecting and commissioning equipment. AV integration covers the wider job of designing how sound, video, control and acoustics work together, coordinating with other trades and configuring everything as one system. Sound Creations does both, so one team is responsible from design to support.

**Can you integrate equipment we already have?**
Yes, depending on your needs. We assess what you have, keep what still performs and design around it, adding only what is needed. We can also repair or service equipment we did not supply before it is integrated into the new system.
